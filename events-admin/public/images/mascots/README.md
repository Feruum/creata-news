# Studyverse mascot variants

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the existing Studyverse mascot reference. WebP delivery files preserve transparency (768 × 768).

Common prompt: preserve the canonical round ivory head, purple three-lobed crest, huge glossy black oval eyes with white highlights, thick short brows, purple cheek patches, small surprised mouth, rounded ivory body, fine black halftone texture and purple rim. Same recognizable 3D toy character; single centered bust with complete crest, head, shoulders, hands and prop; soft studio light, transparent background, no text, logo or watermark.

Variants:

- `welcome.webp`: gently waving hello with one small rounded hand, friendly and curious.
- `scholar.webp`: holding a small closed violet book against its chest with two rounded hands; no writing.
- `star.webp`: holding a small softly glowing warm gold five-point star against its chest with two rounded hands.

The admin editor uploads the chosen preset through the normal achievement-image RPC and EMS storage. Definitions store the resulting storage URL; they do not depend on a local preview server.
