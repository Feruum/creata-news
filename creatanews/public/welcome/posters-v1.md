> A production texture atlas of eight square illustrated event posters for the floating cards on the Sunday Open welcome website. One flat rectangular image with exactly four equal square panels across and two down, edge to edge, no gutters or borders. Aspect ratio 2:1. Sophisticated playful contemporary event poster art, recognizable subjects, bold editorial typography and tactile graphic collage. Cohesive palette: deep blue #2C3887, cyan #299CCC, yellow #FFD536, white and near-black. Independent panels in reading order: AI & TECH (friendly chrome robot), DESIGN (folded cyan/yellow paper), CHESS (yellow knight and white king on blue chessboard), ENGLISH (conversation bubbles), WORKSHOP (drawing hands and pencil), RUN CLUB (running legs and blue track), TALKS (microphone and blurred audience), CREATE (electronics workbench). Only the title appears on each panel. No dates, locations, numbers, web addresses, logos or watermark.
